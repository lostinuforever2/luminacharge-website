01 / PRODUCT CONCEPT

The Luminacharge
project

A portable power bank with a built-in LED light, developed as a product concept for my AIT work.

Purpose

The purpose of Luminacharge is to offer a convenient portable device that combines power-bank charging with an LED light. The idea focuses on usefulness, portability and supporting everyday needs.

Who is it for?

It is intended for students and everyday users who may need to charge a device while away from a power outlet and would also find a small light useful.

KEY FEATURES

  • Portable power-bank concept
  • Built-in LED light
  • Two practical functions in one device
  • Designed around everyday convenience

Feature descriptions reflect the product concept; no unverified technical specifications are claimed.

Design focus

A clean, modern presentation that communicates the product idea clearly and makes the information easy to navigate.
